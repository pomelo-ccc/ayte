### Features & Improvements
- **Auto-Resume Interrupted Sessions After Update Restart**: When Ayte restarts to install an update, it automatically snapshots actively running user sessions (including any pending queued messages, while safely excluding idle, archived, and approval-waiting sessions) and automatically continues them after the updated build launches, with a startup toast notification, a quit-dialog reassurance note, and a toggle in Settings → Updates (`update.resumeInterruptedSessions`, enabled by default).
- **Grouped Collapsible Settings Navigation**: Organized the Settings left navigation rail into 5 collapsible categories (Preferences, Models & Accounts, Capabilities & Interaction, Connections & Extensions, System) with automatic expansion while searching.
